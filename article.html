Calorie Deficit: The Foundation of Fat Loss

A calorie deficit means your body uses more energy than you consume over time. Sustainable implementation matters more than extreme restriction.

What is a calorie deficit?

Energy balance describes the relationship between energy consumed from food and energy used by the body. A sustained deficit generally results in weight loss.

Protein and resistance training

Resistance training and adequate protein can help support lean mass during fat loss. Individual needs vary; a common active-adult starting range is around 1.6–2.0 g/kg/day.

Sleep matters

Sleep supports recovery, appetite regulation and exercise performance. A consistent routine is a useful habit to build alongside nutrition.

Can you lose belly fat only?

Ab exercises strengthen abdominal muscles, but they do not reliably cause selective fat loss from the stomach. Overall fat loss changes body-fat stores.

Steps and movement

Walking and everyday movement contribute to energy expenditure and may be easier to sustain than very intense cardio.

Cardio or weights?

Both can be useful. Resistance training helps strength and muscle retention, while cardio supports cardiovascular fitness and can contribute to energy expenditure.
