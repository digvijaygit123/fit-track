Fat-loss nutrition basics

Build meals around a protein source, vegetables or fruit, mostly minimally processed foods, and portions that fit your energy needs. Avoid extreme restriction and focus on a routine you can sustain.

If you have a medical condition, take medication affected by diet, are pregnant, or have a history of an eating disorder, consult a qualified healthcare professional before changing your diet.
